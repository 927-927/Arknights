In 黑流树海 every currency is priced against one scarce thing: the step. This ledger reconstructs the exchange rates from the game's own menus and shops, then runs an exact route optimiser and a fog-of-war player on all 43 real map layouts to measure what a step, a part, a box slot and a piece of information are actually worth.

在黑流树海里，所有资源最终都要和一种稀缺之物比价：每一步行动力。本页从游戏自己的选项菜单和商店价格里还原出各资源的汇率，再在全部 43 张真实地图模板上运行精确的路线最优解和“迷雾中”玩家模拟，量化一步行动力、一个加工品、一格零件箱和一份视野信息到底值多少。

The ledger账本 货币与汇率

Eleven currencies, one bottleneck十一种货币，一个瓶颈

A run moves value between AP (行动力), hope (希望), ingots (源石锭), collectibles, three kinds of parts, box slots, carry slots and target life. Some conversions are hard-coded. Most are not, but the designers reveal their own prices every time they offer a menu of alternatives: an option nobody would pick gets cut in testing, so the options on one menu should be worth roughly the same.

一局游戏里，价值在行动力、希望、源石锭、收藏品、三类零件、零件箱格、可携带干员位和目标生命之间流动。少数换算是写死的；多数没有明码标价，但设计者每次给出“多选一”菜单，就等于公开了自己的定价：没人会选的选项在测试中会被砍掉，所以同一菜单里的选项价值应当大致相等。

The menus price an AP at about 1.6 hope (3.7 ingots). The exit pays 1 hope. The simulation says a step spent on the map returns 7 IE. AP is the one currency the game systematically undervalues at the exit.

菜单给 1 点行动力的定价约为 1.6 希望（3.7 源石锭），出口只给 1 希望，而模拟显示花在地图上的一步能带来约 7 IE。行动力是唯一一种在出口被系统性低估的货币。

Geometry几何 地图模板

43 layouts, and the budget each one gives you43 张模板，以及每张给你的预算

Every zone is drawn from a fixed set of templates: 3 for floor Ⅰ and 10 each for Ⅱ–Ⅴ. The grid sizes are 3×5, 4×5, 5×7, 5×8 and 5×10. The layouts below come from MAA's map-template file, which its authors built for automated runs. Each zone's AP is fixed (5, 6, 7, 8, 8), and walking costs one AP per node, so the distance from the start to the nearest exit decides how much of the budget is free.

每个区域都从一组固定模板中抽取：第Ⅰ层 3 张，第Ⅱ–Ⅴ层各 10 张，网格尺寸依次为 3×5、4×5、5×7、5×8、5×10。下列布局来自 MAA 为自动化而整理的地图模板文件。每层行动力固定（5、6、7、8、8），步行每经过一个节点耗 1 点，所以起点到最近出口的距离决定了预算里有多少可自由支配。

Two structural facts drive almost everything that follows. The maps are nearly trees (0–8 loops each), so visiting a side branch costs 2 AP, out and back. And on the boss floors the boss is often farther away than the budget.

后文几乎所有结论都源于两个结构事实：地图接近树形（每张只有 0–8 个环），去一个岔路要花 2 点（去和回）；首领层的首领常常比预算还远。

Price of a step一步的价格 行动力的边际价值

What one more AP is worth, floor by floor逐层看：多 1 点行动力值多少

For each floor we sampled hundreds of zones (random template, random contents) and solved each one exactly at budgets from 2 below to 5 above the base. The same zones were also played by a fog-of-war player who replans after every step and only sees what the game shows. The marginal value of an AP is the average gain from one more AP in the same zone.

我们对每层抽样数百个区域（随机模板、随机内容），在基础预算 −2 到 +5 的范围内逐一精确求解。同样的区域也交给“迷雾玩家”去走：它每走一步重新规划，只看得到游戏实际显示的信息。行动力的边际价值，就是同一区域多给 1 点行动力带来的平均收益。

Node values are in ingot-equivalents and are an assumption (they are adjustable in the route lab). The exit converts AP at 1 hope = 3.33 IE, from 桑尼的邀请's 3 hope = 10 ingots.

节点价值以源石锭当量计，属于假设（可在路线实验中调整）。出口把行动力按 1 希望 = 3.33 IE 回收，依据是“桑尼的邀请”中 3 希望 = 10 源石锭。

Does the verdict survive other assumptions?换一套假设，结论还成立吗？

Rule规则

If the best fresh node you can reach is worth more than about a hope per step it costs, walk to it. Bank AP only when the map has nothing left within reach, or when the remaining nodes are dangerous.

只要能到达的最佳新节点每一步价值超过约 1 希望，就走过去。只有当范围内已无可取之物、或剩下的节点太危险时，才把行动力留到出口。

Exits出口 险路尽头 · 险路小径 · 追猎

Three ways out, priced三种离开方式的定价

险路尽头 pays 1 hope per leftover AP plus an ordinary movement part. 险路小径 (the 三重身 cat event) carries the AP into the next zone plus a rarer part. The pursuit starts when AP hits zero away from an exit. On ordinary floors it pays one recruit ticket and risks the run. On boss floors the pursuit is the boss itself (for example 痛苦将息, the pursuit version of 永无安宁), with normal rewards.

险路尽头：每点剩余行动力换 1 希望，外加一个普通加工品。险路小径（“三重身”猫事件）：剩余行动力带入下一区域，外加一个较稀有的加工品。追猎：行动力在出口外归零时触发；普通层只奖励一张招募券，而且可能直接输掉整局；首领层的追猎就是首领战本身（例如永无安宁的追猎版“痛苦将息”），奖励正常。

So the hidden exit should be judged by the next floor's step price, and the boss floors change the question from "can I reach the exit" to "is one more node worth facing the pursuit version of the boss".

所以，隐藏出口要按下一层的行动力价格来评估；而在首领层，问题从“能不能走到出口”变成了“多拿一个节点，值不值得去打追猎版首领”。

Rule规则

On floors Ⅱ and Ⅳ, a reachable 险路小径 beats 险路尽头 unless you need hope at once to recruit. On floors Ⅲ and Ⅴ, plan to spend every AP. Walk into the boss early only if the pursuit version (猎犬proto joining, its healing at 保密等级 15) costs you more than a couple of good nodes.

第Ⅱ、Ⅳ层若够得着险路小径，除非急需希望招募，否则它优于险路尽头。第Ⅲ、Ⅴ层按花光所有行动力来规划；只有当追猎版首领（猎犬proto 加入，保密等级 15 时还会给首领回血）的代价超过两三个好节点时，才提前走进首领。

Movement parts移动零件 加工品

Almost every jump pays for itself几乎每一次跳跃都能回本

A part move skips the rule that you must finish every node you walk through, and most cost 1 AP or none. We solved the same zones with and without each part (used on that floor, full information) and took the difference.

加工品移动可以绕开“经过的节点必须完成”的规则，而且多数只耗 1 点行动力甚至不耗。我们把同一批区域在有、无该加工品的情况下分别求解（在该层用掉，完全信息），取两者之差。

Show all floors查看所有楼层

The ranking follows reach and price of use. 简易遥控器 moves for free and adds 3 AP, so it is worth about four ordinary nodes. Free long jumps (喷气背包, 重弹簧) come next. Multi-use parts (标准引擎, 试作外骨骼, 结构性原理) gain most on the larger floors Ⅳ–Ⅴ, where their extra uses find targets. 紧急逃生座椅 is only worth carrying into a floor where the exit is far, which on our sample means floor Ⅴ almost never: the pursuit already ends the zone for free.

排名取决于射程和每次使用的代价。“简易遥控器”免费移动还加 3 点行动力，约等于四个普通节点；其次是免费的远程跳跃（喷气背包、重弹簧）。多次使用的标准引擎、试作外骨骼、结构性原理在更大的第Ⅳ–Ⅴ层收益最高，额外的次数在那里才找得到目标。紧急逃生座椅只在出口很远的楼层才值得带，而在我们的样本里，第Ⅴ层几乎用不上它：追猎本来就会免费结束这一层。

The merchant行商 秘境行商 · 自然物

Natural items are an investment portfolio自然物是一个投资组合

秘境行商 sells natural items at twice their estimate. Their estimates then grow with what you do: battles, moves, reveals, recruits, merchant visits. Sold later, they return ingots. The table uses the action counts our fog player actually produced on each floor, so growth is tied to realistic routes.

秘境行商按估价的两倍出售自然物，此后它们的估价会随你的行动增长：作战、移动、揭示、招募、进入行商……之后卖掉就能换回源石锭。下表使用迷雾玩家在各层实际产生的行动次数，使增长与真实路线挂钩。

Two readings are uncertain. 雾滚草 says +1 "each time node information is revealed": per reveal event or per node revealed. And recruits per zone are not logged by the simulation; the table assumes 1.5. Use the controls to change both.

有两处不确定：雾滚草写的是“每次揭示节点信息时估价+1”，可以理解为按“揭示事件”计，也可以按“揭示的节点数”计；另外模拟没有记录每区域的招募次数，表中假设为 1.5。两者都可以用下面的控件修改。

Trading over a whole run整局交易模拟

A Monte Carlo trader visits 秘境行商 on floors Ⅱ–Ⅴ, sees offers drawn from MAA's recorded shop layouts and offer rates, buys whatever is expected to grow past its price, and liquidates on floor Ⅴ. It earns 10 ingots per zone from other sources. With one visit per floor it makes about 25 ingots per run, roughly a tenth of what routing is worth.

蒙特卡洛交易者在第Ⅱ–Ⅴ层进入秘境行商，货架按 MAA 记录的商店布局和出现率随机生成；它买入预计能涨过售价的自然物，并在第Ⅴ层全部卖出。每区域另有 10 源石锭的其他收入。每层进一次时，它每局约赚 25 源石锭，大约是路线价值的十分之一。

Rule规则

Plan routes through 秘境行商 on floors Ⅱ–Ⅳ before you buy capacity. Buy growth items early, because their growth compounds with every battle, move and recruit that follows. Sell everything on floor Ⅴ, as MAA's own trade profile does. Extra capacity only pays when the box is nearly full of movement parts; 多边贸易's +8 per three sold is the bigger gain.

先让路线经过第Ⅱ–Ⅳ层的秘境行商，再考虑扩容。增值型自然物要早买，之后的每场作战、每次移动和招募都会让它继续增值。第Ⅴ层全部卖出，MAA 自己的交易配置也是这样做的。只有零件箱快被加工品塞满时，扩容才划算；多边贸易“卖 3 个 +8”的奖励才是更大的收益。

Information信息 视野 · 羽瞰点 · 模板

How much the fog costs迷雾到底让你损失多少

The fog is mild. Nodes you have not reached still show whether they are hostile (未知的凶戾) or not (未知的诡秘), and exits are shown in advance below 保密等级 3. Our fog player values unseen nodes at their category average and replans after every reveal. The gap to the full-information optimum is the value of perfect information.

迷雾其实很温和：没走到的节点仍会显示是“未知的凶戾”还是“未知的诡秘”，保密等级 3 以下出口也会提前显示。迷雾玩家把未揭示节点按其类别的平均值估价，每次揭示后重新规划。它与完全信息最优解之间的差距，就是完全信息的价值。

Perfect information is worth less than one ordinary node per floor. That is why 襁褓骏鹰 (reveal floors Ⅰ–Ⅱ plus 1 hope) loses to 襁褓天马, and why 洛伦茨之眼 is a weak concept part. Where information does matter is avoiding mistakes: without template knowledge at 保密等级 3+, the fog player fell into an ordinary pursuit in 8% of floor Ⅱ zones.

完全信息每层的价值还不到一个普通节点。所以襁褓骏鹰（第Ⅰ–Ⅱ层全图视野 + 1 希望）不如襁褓天马，洛伦茨之眼也是偏弱的概念体。信息真正重要的地方在于避免失误：保密等级 3+ 时，不认得模板的迷雾玩家在 8% 的第Ⅱ层区域里误触了普通追猎。

Method方法 数据与假设

Where the numbers come from数据从哪里来

Data

  • Map layouts: 43 templates (grid size, start, exits, every edge) from MaaAssistantArknights resource/roguelike/BlackFlow/map_templates.json (AGPL-3.0). Exits in these files are where 险路尽头 or 险路恶敌 sit.
  • Part prices and shop layouts: MAA scrap_market.json and shopping.json: buy prices, shelf layouts with probabilities, offer rates, refresh costs 4/8/12/16.
  • Rules, parts, events, squads, difficulty: BWIKI 沉沦者的黑流树海 and its 引擎配件目录 and 调查记录 pages; PRTS for AP per zone, hope costs (6★ recruit 6, promote 3) and 应急助力 prices.
  • Reveal rule: nodes are visible when nothing unfinished lies between you and them, as in BWIKI's 地图与移动 and MAA's reveal_through_transparent_nodes.

Assumptions (not published by the game)

  • Node mix: relative weights per node type, with the floor restrictions PRTS lists (no 安全的角落 on Ⅰ, no 先行一步 on Ⅰ or Ⅴ, and so on). One 诡意行商 on floor Ⅰ, as MAA's routes assume.
  • Node values in ingot-equivalents: battle 6, emergency 12, encounter 9, wish 12, safe corner 10, go ahead 10. The sensitivity table and the lab sliders test these.
  • Hidden exit: 50% of non-boss zones have one. Tunnels: 35% of larger zones have a pair. Pursuit: −30 IE on ordinary floors, −6 IE for the boss pursuit version.
  • Trading: recruits 1.5 per zone, 10 ingots of other income per zone, shop type chosen uniformly among MAA's three layouts.

Limits

  • The simulation prices outcomes in ingot-equivalents. It does not model combat, so it cannot say whether a team survives the extra battles a route takes.
  • 理想源 and 实托邦, 黑潭, 居民据点 and events with branching follow-ups are not simulated.
  • Long-range parts try only the most valuable targets (10, or 6 for 3-use parts), which can slightly understate them.

Reproduce

The site is static. node research/scripts/build_data.js rebuilds the data file from the sources, and node research/scripts/experiments.js reruns every experiment (about 20 minutes; pass 0.1 for a quick check).

数据

  • 地图模板：43 张模板（网格尺寸、起点、出口和全部连线）来自 MaaAssistantArknights 的 resource/roguelike/BlackFlow/map_templates.json（AGPL-3.0）。文件中的出口即险路尽头或险路恶敌的位置。
  • 零件价格与货架布局：MAA 的 scrap_market.json 和 shopping.json：售价、带概率的货架布局、出现率、刷新费用 4/8/12/16。
  • 规则、零件、事件、分队、难度：BWIKI「沉沦者的黑流树海」及其“引擎配件目录”“调查记录”页面；每层行动力、希望消耗（六星招募 6、进阶 3）和应急助力价格来自 PRTS。
  • 揭示规则：当你与某节点之间没有未完成的节点时，该节点可见，与 BWIKI“地图与移动”及 MAA 的 reveal_through_transparent_nodes 一致。

假设（游戏未公布）

  • 节点构成：各类节点的相对权重，并遵守 PRTS 列出的楼层限制（第Ⅰ层没有安全的角落，第Ⅰ、Ⅴ层没有先行一步等）。按 MAA 路线的假定，第Ⅰ层有一个诡意行商。
  • 节点价值（源石锭当量）：作战 6、紧急作战 12、不期而遇 9、得偿所愿 12、安全的角落 10、先行一步 10。敏感性表格和实验滑块用于检验这些取值。
  • 隐藏出口：50% 的非首领区域有一个。曲折密道：35% 的较大区域有一对。追猎：普通层 −30 IE，追猎版首领 −6 IE。
  • 交易：每区域招募 1.5 次，每区域另有 10 源石锭收入，商店类型在 MAA 的三种布局中均匀抽取。

局限

  • 模拟以源石锭当量为结果定价，不模拟战斗本身，因此无法判断队伍能否扛住路线额外带来的战斗。
  • 没有模拟理想源与实托邦、黑潭、“居民”据点以及带后续分支的事件。
  • 远程加工品只尝试价值最高的若干目标（3 次使用的为 6 个，其余为 10 个），可能略微低估它们。
